Tomas Video

A private, personal tool.

Tomas Video is a private tool used only by its owner. It saves videos that the owner's team shares in a private Telegram group into the owner's own Google Drive, so they can be edited into short marketing videos for the owner's holiday rental units in Kuching, Sarawak.

It is not offered to the public, and it does not access anyone else's Google account.
